## 方法与边界

Candidate ordering is based on public signal strength from official hospital pages, center pages, public news, guideline participation, disease-specific center status, and disclosed scale metrics. It is not a clinical ranking.

This dataset is for product demo, source registry, and research triage. It is not medical advice, hospital endorsement, or treatment-quality ranking. Surgery and patient-volume metrics preserve period, scope, definition, and source status where available.
